= Scale to the Moon

This lab section is a discussion exercise for other scenarios you could expect
to encounter—and likely solve—with OpenShift Virtualization and Ansible
Automation Platform.

Take a few minutes to discuss with a nearby lab mate who has also completed the
lab. When you are ready, call over a proctor and pose your idea to them.

[[ex_scenarios]]
== Example Scenarios Created by the Lab Team

=== Scenario 1

Automating virtual machine deployments across multiple managed OpenShift
clusters using Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management for Kubernetes, Ansible
Automation Platform, GitOps, and OpenShift Virtualization.

=== Scenario 2

Using event-driven Ansible in Ansible Automation Platform to collect virtual
machine metrics, then trigger automated hot-plug of resources when utilization
hits certain thresholds.

=== Scenario 3

Enabling the cluster descheduler in OpenShift so workloads automatically
re-balance when new resources are available—for example after adding a node in
the scaling scenario, so overloaded nodes live-migrate VMs onto the new node.
